# Research update — 4 November 2026

The litigation tracker was reviewed through 4 November 2026. The latest court record added is dated 31 October. This is a dated source review, not a live court docket or a claim of exhaustive coverage.

## Changes in the litigation record

- **14 July:** the Supreme Court dismissed the specified State SLPs and the Commission’s Diary 36073/2024 as withdrawn. The order expressly names Diary 45526/2025 and SLP(C) 26497/2025 too. Other affected persons may approach the Court. Withdrawal is not a merits decision, and does not establish disposal of unnamed party appeals. [Court-text mirror](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/16492254/).
- **12 August:** *Shreyasi Dhang*, MAT 1320/2026, addresses certificate validity after withdrawal. Its paragraph 25 covers the stated certificate-issuance periods and fresh certification for eligible retained classes. The mirror has inconsistent historical dates; use the separately archived 17 June and 28 July 2025 orders for those dates. [Court-text mirror](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/60350638/).
- **1 October:** in Diary 41300/2026 with Diary 50605/2026, the Supreme Court issued notice, directed tagging with Diary 54999/2026, and made appointments subject to the result, with the condition in appointment letters. It directed listing on **23 November 2026**. This is a future listing direction as of this review; the order does not record a general stay or restoration of 17% reservation. [Court-text mirror](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/147304353/).
- **13 and 15 October:** *Nilanjona Kundu*, WPA 29788/2026, and *Abeda Khatoon*, WPA 30672/2026, concern retained-class SLST candidates who missed category updates. Their representation/verification directions do not grant automatic reservation or reopen editing for everyone. Their representation deadlines have passed. [Nilanjona Kundu](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/100359379/), [Abeda Khatoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/174058171/).
- **31 October:** *Mohabul Islam*, WPA 31572/2026, calls for affidavits on former OBC applicants’ request to update to EWS in primary-teacher recruitment. Intervening steps remain subject to the writ result; EWS conversion has not been decided. [Court-text mirror](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/190165311/).

Six local PDF reproductions preserve the court text for offline reading. Each prominently identifies itself as a **court-text mirror, not an official/certified court PDF**. Pagination is local, not the original court pagination. Source URLs, retrieval date, page counts and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `case_manifest.json` and `case_pdfs/README.md`.

The tracker now counts records by explicit type rather than labelling entries as “pending” or “resolved” through keyword matching. A procedural report, historic order and party paper book cannot reliably establish current case status.
