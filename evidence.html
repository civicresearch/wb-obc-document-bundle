The archive contains the core 2010, 2025, and 2026 notifications and a substantial public-hearing notice set. The remaining procedural gap is the West Bengal Commission for Backward Classes record behind the notices: benchmark-survey memos, class-wise advice reports, survey data, and later court records around the 2025 survey challenge.

Found online from original sources

Official BCW order indexes, official WBCBC public-hearing notice list, and the official 2025 notification-wise OBC list.

Partly found

Benchmark-survey memos are quoted or mirrored online, but the original Commission PDF locations still need recovery or download from the department/Commission if available.

Not yet archived

Class-wise Commission advice/survey reports, the benchmark-survey memo trail, complete court filings/orders after the 2025 survey challenge, and original source pages for hearing notices currently represented only by archived scans.
