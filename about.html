About this project

A source-led record of West Bengal OBC policy

This independent research archive brings together publicly available notifications, statutes, public-hearing notices, policy reports, and selected court records concerning Other Backward Classes (OBCs) in West Bengal, and publishes them online for free public access.

Purpose

Make the record easier to examine

The project is designed as a chronological research desk. It helps readers trace a claim from a timeline entry to a notification, a hearing notice, a policy source, or a court record without treating summaries as a substitute for the original document.

Scope

Policy, process, and litigation

The archive covers the statutory framework, State OBC-list changes, reservation and roster records, the 2025–26 notification sequence, public hearings, and a growing litigation tracker. It does not claim to be a complete government archive.

Use with care

Research aid, not legal advice

Some records are text-extracted or copied from publicly accessible mirrors. Court status, operative directions, and notification validity should always be confirmed from the issuing authority or the relevant court.

How records are handledWhat this means
Published onlineThe archive is hosted on the open web and served straight from the repository — no download or installation needed.
Source labelsEntries distinguish official downloads, scanned/text-extracted records, and mirror copies that need verification.
Large case bundlesVery large filings are catalogued but not added by default; this keeps the site fast and practical to maintain.
Corrections and additionsUse the Add Record guide's fields to record date, authority, status, source URL, and the affected notification or case.
Source principle: where this site summarizes a record, open the linked document and treat the primary source as controlling. The project does not endorse any political position or make findings about any community.

Who maintains this?

This archive is built and maintained by a small team of volunteers, supported by the Civic Research Group community. It is an ongoing effort: notifications, court records, and policy reports are added as they are collected and verified, and the coverage keeps growing. If you hold a document, a correction, or a gap we have missed, the Contribute page is the fastest way to help improve the record.
