NOTIFICATION
Date : 14.10.2026
The Backward Classes Welfare (BCW) Department, Government of West Bengal has issued Notification No. 944-
BCW/MR-23/2026 dated 18th May, 2026 of Backward Classes Welfare (BCW) Department withdrawing of all the
previous notifications issued in connection with OBC reservation in 2025. Another notification bearing No. 945-
BCW/MR-23/2026 dated 18th May, 2026 of Backward Classes Welfare (BCW) Department, Government of West
Bengal notifying the State List of OBCs comprising 66 (sixty six) classes for availing 7% reservation in services and
posts under the Government of West Bengal has also been issued by the Government in consonance of the judgement
passed by the Hon'ble High Court, Calcutta dated 22.05.2024.
In this context, it is hereby notified for information to all eligible candidates for recruitment of Assistant Teachers in
Govt. Aided / Govt. Sponsored Primary / Junior Basic Schools in the State in accordance with the provisions of "West
Bengal Primary School Teachers Recruitment Rules, 2016" (amended upto date) in connection with the notification
vide Memo No. 2141/WBBPE/2025/59R-09/2024 dated 25.09.2025, that an online portal for declaration of OBC
Class will remain active from 14.10.2026 to 31.10.2026 (11:59 p.m.).
In pursuance to the latest notification, candidates who have applied under OBC-A/OBC-B category for recruitment to
the post of Assistant Teachers in Primary Schools vide Memo No. 2141/WBBPE/2025/59R-09/2024 dated
25.09.2025 are advised to update/modify their category clearly mentioning their Class (in case of OBCs) on clicking
the link : PRIMARY TEACHER RECRUITMENT 2025 and then click on CATEGORY CORRECTION to be made
available on the websites of West Bengal Board of Primary Education (https://wbbpe.wb.gov.in) within the period
from 14.10.2026 to 31.10.2026 (11:59 p.m.). Candidates who have applied as OBC-A/OBC-B for recruitment to the
post of Assistant Teachers in Primary Schools and their class is now presently included in the latest State List of 66
(sixty six) classes of OBCs are advised to clearly mention their class in the link to get the benefit of reservation under
OBC category as per the latest State List of OBCs. If they fail to do so, they will be treated as Unreserved category.
Candidates who have applied as OBC-A/OBC-B for recruitment to the post of Assistant Teachers in Primary Schools
but whose class is presently excluded from the latest State List of OBCs, may change their category to Un-reserved,
subject to fulfillment of the prescribed conditions for Un-reserved (General) as noted in our Information to Applicant.
If they fail to do so they will be treated as Un-reserved (General) category.
Candidates claiming the aforesaid reservation benefit shall be required to produce a valid original OBC Certificate,
duly issued by the competent authority in the prescribed format and in accordance with the extant Government orders,
at the time of counselling/admission and document verification. Failure to produce the requisite original OBC
Certificate during verification of documents shall result in cancellation of the claimed reservation benefit, and the
candidature shall be considered strictly in accordance with the applicable/extant rules of the Board and the concerned
authority.
No request for further declaration, modification or inclusion of OBC Class shall be entertained after closure of
the portal as edit option will be available till the closer of the portal. Therefore, all eligible candidates are
advised to complete the declaration process well before the last date.
