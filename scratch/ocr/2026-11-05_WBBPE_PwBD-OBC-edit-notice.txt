Memo No. 3747/WBBPE/2026/2024L-11/26
NOTIFICATION
Date : 05.11.2026
Pertaining to edit option for updating PwBD (Persons with Benchmark Disability) category details
and OBC status for the candidates for recruitment of Assistant Teachers in Govt. Aided/ Govt.
Sponsored Primary/ Junior Basic Schools in the State
In continuation of our earlier notification no. 3536/WBBPE/2026/59R-09/2024 dated 14.10.2026 and in
view of the revised vacancy provided to the West Bengal Board of Primary Education, consequent to the
Notification No. 944-BCW/MR-23/2026 dated 18th May, 2026 and the Notification No. 945-BCW/MR-
23/2026 dated 18th May, 2026 of Backward Classes Welfare (BCW) Department, Government of West
Bengal, it is hereby notified for information to all eligible candidates for recruitment of Assistant Teachers
in Govt. Aided / Govt. Sponsored Primary / Junior Basic Schools in the State in accordance with the
provisions of "West Bengal Primary School Teachers Recruitment Rules, 2016" (amended upto date) in
connection with the notification vide Memo No. 2141/WBBPE/2025/59R-09/2024 dated 25.09.2025 and in
view of the revised vacancy provided to the West Bengal Board of Primary Education, consequent to the
Notification No. 944-BCW/MR-23/2026 dated 18th May, 2026 and the Notification No. 945-BCW/MR-
23/2026 dated 18th May, 2026 of Backward Classes Welfare (BCW) Department, Government of West
Bengal, that an online portal for updation of PwBD (Persons with Benchmark Disability) category will
remain active from 05.11.2026 to 09.11.2026 (11:59 p.m.).
In pursuance to the latest notification, candidates who have earlier applied under PwD category for
recruitment to the post of Assistant Teachers in Primary Schools vide Memo No. 2141/WBBPE/2025/59R-
09/2024 dated 25.09.2025 are advised to update/modify their category (UR-PwBD or SC-PwBD) on
clicking the link : PRIMARY TEACHER RECRUITMENT 2025 and then click on CATEGORY
CORRECTION to be made available on the websites of West Bengal Board of Primary Education
(https://wbbpe.wb.gov.in) within the period from 05.11.2026 to 09.11.2026 (11:59 p.m.) to get the benefit
of reservation under PwBD category.
Candidates claiming the aforesaid reservation benefit shall be required to produce a valid original PwBD
Certificate, duly issued by the competent authority in the prescribed format and in accordance with the
extant Government orders, at the time of counselling/admission and document verification. Failure to
produce the requisite original PwBD Certificate during verification of documents shall result in cancellation
of the claimed reservation benefit, and the candidature shall be considered strictly in accordance with the
applicable/extant rules of the Board and the concerned authority.
And
Candidates who have not declared their OBC status will also be able to update their status during the period
from 05.11.2026 to 09.11.2026 (up to 11:59 p.m.) in order to avail themselves of the benefit of reservation
under the OBC category.
No request for further extension shall be entertained after closure of the portal as edit option will be
available till the closer of the portal. Therefore, all eligible candidates are advised to complete the
declaration process well before the last date.
