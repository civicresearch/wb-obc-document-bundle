GOVERNMENT OF WEST BENGAL
BACKWARD CLASSES WELFARE DEPARTMENT
ADMINISTRATIVE BUILDING, 5th FLOOR
DJ 4, SECTOR II, SALT LAKE, KOLKATA

No. 1060/Secy/BCW/7P-63/2024
Dated: 07.08.2026
WHEREAS, the Hon'ble Division Bench of the High Court at Calcutta,
vide Judgment dated 22nd May, 2024 passed in the matter of WPO 60 of
2011 with WPA 22145 of 2010 with WPA 8844 of 2020 with WPO 1160 of
2013 with WPO 578 of 2012, struck down, inter alia, 113 (one hundred and
thirteen) classes out of the then enlisted 179 (one hundred and seventy
nine) classes in the State List of Other Backward Classes (OBCs), which had
been included from time to time for the reason stated therein;
AND WHEREAS, in the said judgment of Hon'ble Division Bench
remaining 66 (sixty six) classes included in the State List of OBCs prior to
2010 were not interfered with;
AND WHEREAS, the Hon'ble Division Bench further struck down the
then categorisation of enlisted OBCs as OBC (Category-A) and OBC
(Category-B) ;
AND WHEREAS, the Hon'ble Division Bench further struck down the
distribution of the total 17% reservation for OBCs, i.e. 10% for OBC
(Category-A) and 7% for OBC (Category-B), leaving only 7% OBC reservation
for such 66 classes;
AND WHEREAS, the Hon'ble Division Bench of the High Court at
Calcutta further directed that "The services of citizens from the 77
classes and the 37 classes (added in exercise of Section 16) struck
down above, who were already in the service of the State, or have
already availed the benefit of reservation or have succeeded in any
[Page 2]
selection process in the State, shall not be affected by the reason of
this judgment.";
AND WHEREAS, being aggrieved by the aforesaid Judgment dated
22nd May, 2024, the State of West Bengal filed Special Leave Petitions being
SLP(C) Nos. 017751—017755/2024 before the Hon'ble Supreme Court of
India, and the West Bengal Commission for Backward Classes also filed
SLP(C) Diary No. 36073 of 2024 against the said Judgment;
AND WHEREAS, after mentioning the matter before the Hon'ble
Supreme Court of India and on the basis of recommendations of the West
Bengal Commission for Backward Classes, this Department, with due
approval of the State Cabinet, published five Notifications bearing Nos. 917—
BCW/MR-33/2025 dated 8th May, 2025, 1056-BCW/MR-33/2025 (Pt. 1)
dated 27th May, 2025, 1057-BCW/MR-38/2025 dated 27th May, 2025,
1106-BCW/MR-33/2025 dated 3rd June, 2025 and 1107-BCW/MR-
38/2025 dated 3rd June, 2025, read with Corrigendum Notification No.
1172-BCW/MR-38/2025 dated 12th June,
2025, regarding sub-
categorisation of the enlisted backward classes, inclusion of new classes in
the State List of OBCs and increase as well as distribution of percentage of
reservation for OBCs in the State;
AND WHEREAS, all the aforesaid Notifications were challenged before
the Hon'ble High Court at Calcutta and the Hon'ble Division Bench, vide
interim order dated 17th June, 2025 passed in connection with WPA (P) 111
of 2025 with IA No. CAN 1 of 2025 and other connected matters, stayed the
operation of the aforesaid Notifications and all consequential steps taken by
the State Government;
AND WHEREAS, subsequently, the State of West Bengal filed Special
Leave Petitions bearing SLP(C) No. 017422/2025, SLP(C) No. 017528/2025
and SLP(C) Nos. 017973—017974/2025 against the aforesaid order dated
17th June, 2025 before the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India;
[Page 3]
AND WHEREAS, the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India, vide order dated
28th July, 2025, was pleased to stay the operation of the impugned order
dated 17th June, 2025 passed by the Hon'ble High Court at Calcutta;
AND WHEREAS, subsequently, the State Government decided to
comply with the order & Judgment dated 22nd May, 2024 passed by the
Hon'ble High Court at Calcutta and accordingly decided to withdraw all
Special Leave Petitions pending before the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India
in matters relating to OBC reservation;
AND WHEREAS, with due approval of the State Cabinet, this
Department published two fresh Notifications bearing Notification No. 944-
BCW/MR-23/2026 dated 18th May, 2026, for withdrawal of all the aforesaid
previous Notifications issued in connection with OBC reservation in 2025,
and Notification No. 945-BCW/MR-23/2026 dated 18th May, 2026,
notifying the State List of OBCs comprising 66 classes which were not
interfered with in the aforesaid Judgment of the Hon'ble High Court at
Calcutta, for availing 7% reservation in services and posts under the
Government of West Bengal;
AND WHEREAS, the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India, vide order dated
14th July, 2026, disposed of all the aforesaid pending Special Leave
Petitions and dismissed the same as withdrawn;
AND WHEREAS, in the context of reservation for Other Backward
Classes (OBCs) in appointments to services and posts under the
Government of West Bengal, Chairpersons/ Heads of various recruiting
organisations and various head of the departments sought clarification for
implementation of reservation policy in respect of the OBCs in the State of
West Bengal.
[Page 4]
AND WHEREAS, in response thereto, requisite information has been
received from the different recruiting organisations/ authorities concerned
regarding the status of ongoing recruitment processes;
AND WHEREAS, in view of the above, considered opinion was
solicited from the Ld Advocate General, West Bengal and Ld Legal
Remembrancer, West Bengal and opinion of Ld Solicitor General of India
was also solicited through Additional Advocate General, West Bengal
regarding the applicability of the revised reservation policy to recruitment
processes initiated prior to the Judgment dated 22nd May, 2024,
advertisements were issued following the 100-Point Roster of vacancies in
terms of Notification No. 50-Emp/ 1 M-25/98 dated 1st March, 2011 of the
Labour Department, wherein reservation was provided at the rate of 10% for
OBC-A and 7% for OBC-B, applicable to the 179 classes then enlisted in the
State OBC List or initiated on or after 10th June, 2025 and up to 18th
May, 2026, wherein recruitment processes were undertaken pursuant to
Notification Nos. 917-BCW/MR-33/2025 dated 8th May, 2025, 1056-
BCW/MR-33/2025 (Pt. 1) dated 27th May, 2025, 1057-BCW/MR-38/2025
dated 27th May, 2025, 1106-BCW/MR-33/2025 dated 3rd June, 2025 and
1107-BCW / MR-38/2025 dated 3rd June, 2025, read with Corrigendum
Notification No. 1172-BCW/MR-38/2025 dated 12th June, 2025, all
published in the Kolkata Gazette on 10th June, 2025, wherein reservation
was fixed at the rate of 10% for OBC-A and 7% for OBC-B, applicable to the
140 classes included in the State List, read with Notification No. Labr/ 110-
Emp/EC/ 1 M-01/2025 dated 13th June, 2025 of the Labour Department
regarding the Model 100-Point Roster, particularly with respect to the
different stages of recruitment;
AND WHEREAS, the State Government has been advised that the
recruitment process initiated during the periods in question and where
appointment letters have already been issued to the successful candidates
on the basis of the final merit list shall abide by the then reservation policy
of the State Government so far as OBC reservation is concerned. In respect
[Page 5]
of the other cases, the Government has been advised to follow notifications
published vide Nos. 944-BCW/MR-23/2026 dated 18.05.2026 and 945-
BCW / MR-23/2026 dated 18.05.2026 read with Notification No. Labr/85-
Emp/EC/ 1M-01/2025 dated 25.05.2026 of the Labour Department
NOW THEREFORE, all the recruiting agencies/ authorities of the
State of West Bengal are hereby advised to act accordingly while disposing
the ongoing recruitment processes.
Secretary to the Govt. of West Bengal
