These are not West Bengal notification files, but they explain the policy architecture used to understand OBC identification, Muslim OBCs, affirmative action, and evidence requirements.

1980 · Mandal Commission

Criteria for identifying socially and educationally backward classes

The Mandal Commission is the baseline framework for OBC policy. Its relevant section groups backwardness indicators under social, educational, and economic heads, uses a weighted score, and treats 11 points out of 22 as the threshold in its survey method. It also notes that caste-based indicators needed a separate approach for non-Hindu backward communities.

  • Use here: evaluate whether a notification or class-wise report explains social, educational, and economic backwardness.
  • Relevant chapters: socio-educational field survey, criteria of backwardness, identification of OBCs, recommendations.

2006 · Sachar Committee

Muslim OBCs and affirmative action

The Sachar report supplies the national evidence layer for Muslim social, economic, and educational conditions. Chapter 10 is especially relevant because it treats Muslim OBCs as a distinct analytical category and compares education, employment, poverty, landholding, and access to entitlements.

  • Use here: distinguish religious identity from backwardness evidence and locate Muslim OBC disadvantage in measurable indicators.
  • Relevant chapter: “The Muslim OBCs and Affirmative Action.”

2015 · West Bengal research

Muslim OBCs in West Bengal

This research note links Mandal, Sachar, and West Bengal lists. It discusses Muslim OBC population estimates, classification issues, implementation problems, and the state’s Category-A/Category-B framework.

  • Use here: contextualize West Bengal-specific Muslim OBC entries and gaps in identification or benefit delivery.
  • Relevant sections: Muslim OBC population, proforma/listing in West Bengal, implementation problems.

2011 · NCBC advice compilation

Central OBC advice concerning West Bengal

This 80-page compilation begins with National Commission for Backward Classes advice concerning West Bengal communities. It is included as a source collection for Central-list context; it does not itself determine the current State OBC list.

  • Use here: trace Central Commission advice and compare it carefully with State notifications.
  • Source care: archived scan; confirm the original NCBC record before relying on individual pages.

Commission survey proforma

Block-level evidence template

The West Bengal Commission for Backward Classes proforma asks for block-wise population, social background, education, occupation, representation, and comparative local information. It is a useful example of the evidence trail that should accompany a class-identification exercise.

2015 · National Commission for Backward Classes

Sub-categorisation within OBCs

This NCBC report was prepared for the Central List and considers methods, categories, and related issues for sub-categorisation. It is national policy context, not authority for the West Bengal State List or a substitute for a State notification.

  • Use here: compare proposed sub-categorisation methods with the evidence and process used in State exercises.
  • Source care: archived scan dated 2 March 2015; verify the primary NCBC publication before relying on specific recommendations.
