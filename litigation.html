  22. 12 Aug 2026

    Calcutta High Court: Shreyasi Dhang certificate-validity judgment

    In Shreyasi Dhang, the Division Bench rejected the admission-related appeal and held the May–June 2025 re-declaration unlawful. Paragraph 25 treats certificates issued during 8 May–12 June 2025 and 10 June 2025–18 May 2026 as void from inception and requires fresh certification for eligible pre-2010 classes. The judgment records that withdrawal of the State’s SLPs extinguished the interim stay and revived the High Court order. It must now be read with the fresh Supreme Court proceedings of 1 October. The mirror contains inconsistent historical dates for the 2025 orders; the archived orders establish 17 June and 28 July 2025.

    Archived court-text mirror (9 pp.) · Source court text Local reproduction; not an official or certified court PDF. Reviewed 4 November 2026.

Litigation support note · not an official order

Legal principles for existing OBC-certificate appointees

A user-supplied bilingual note argues that bona fide employees appointed on government-issued and verified OBC certificates should not face blanket termination merely because a reservation policy or class-list process is later invalidated. Treat this as an argument map: verify every citation against the actual judgment before using it.

Factual premise

No fraud or false claim

The note’s protection argument depends on the employee having acted in good faith, without misrepresentation, and on certificates having been issued and verified by competent government authorities before appointment.

Service finality

Completed appointments

It distinguishes policy validity from appointment validity and says completed recruitment, seniority, promotion, pension, vested rights, and livelihood interests require individualised treatment.

Process safeguard

No blanket cancellation

The note relies on Articles 14, 16, and 21, natural justice, proportionality, legitimate expectation, and administrative fairness to oppose mass termination without hearing.
