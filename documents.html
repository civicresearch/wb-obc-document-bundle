658-L / West Bengal Act VIII of 20262026-07-15legislationframeworkThe amendment revises the statutory definitions, reservation and sub-categorisation provisions, certificate requirements and schedules, and omits section 16. Percentage-setting and sub-categorisation require consultation with the Commission. Published on 15 July; brought into force on 27 July 2026 by BCW/15011/17/1579. The amendment does not itself publish a new class list or restore 17% reservation.Open PDF
659-L / West Bengal Act IX of 20262026-07-15legislationframeworkThe amendment revises member-secretary eligibility and tenure, and substitutes section 9 on inclusion requests, over-/under-inclusion complaints and ordinarily binding Commission advice. Published on 15 July; brought into force on 27 July 2026 by BCW/15011/18/1580. Read with the principal 1993 Act rather than treating the original text as the complete current statute.Open PDF
BCW/15011/17/15792026-07-27commencement notificationframeworkThe Gazette notification appoints 27 July 2026 as the commencement date of the amendment to the 2012 reservation Act. Publication of the Act on 15 July and its commencement on 27 July are separate events.Open PDF
BCW/15011/18/15802026-07-27commencement notificationframeworkThe Gazette notification appoints 27 July 2026 as the commencement date of the amendment to the 1993 Commission Act.Open PDF
1060/Secy/BCW/7P-63/20242026-08-07administrativecurrentBCW Department notification to all recruiting agencies on OBC reservation in recruitments already under way. Where appointment letters were already issued on a final merit list, the reservation policy in force at the time applies (10% OBC-A and 7% OBC-B, for processes begun before 22 May 2024 or between 10 June 2025 and 18 May 2026). All other cases follow Notifications 944 and 945 of 18 May 2026, read with the Labour Department roster notification Labr/85-Emp/EC/1M-01/2025 of 25 May 2026.Open PDF
WBBPE Memo 3536/WBBPE/2026/59R-09/20242026-10-14administrativecurrentWest Bengal Board of Primary Education notice for the 2025 Assistant Teacher recruitment (Memo 2141 of 25 September 2025). A portal open from 14 to 31 October 2026 lets OBC-A and OBC-B applicants name their class if it is in the 66-class list of Notification 945, or move to Unreserved if it is not. Applicants who do nothing are treated as Unreserved, and an original OBC certificate is needed at verification.Open PDF
WBBPE Memo 3747/WBBPE/2026/2024L-11/262026-11-05administrativecurrentWest Bengal Board of Primary Education notice continuing Memo 3536. Because vacancies were revised after Notifications 944 and 945, a portal open from 5 to 9 November 2026 lets candidates with disabilities update their category, and also lets candidates who have not declared OBC status do so to claim OBC reservation. It comes after the High Court's 30 October 2026 order that the October window did not allow fresh applications.Open PDF
Labr/85-Emp/EC/1M-01/20252026-05-25administrativecurrentLabour Department Gazette notification (Kolkata Gazette Extraordinary, 25 May 2026) prescribing the revised Model 100-Point Roster of Vacancies after Notification 945-BCW. OBC now has 7 of the 100 points (points 9, 19, 29, 49, 59, 69 and 89; points 19 and 69 are for exempted-category OBC candidates and point 49 for an OBC ex-serviceman in Group-D posts), matching the 7% reservation. It also sets the EWS, SC, ST, disability, ex-serviceman and sportsperson points, and supersedes Labour Department Notification Labr/20-EMP/EC/1M-01/2025 of 27 January 2026.Open PDF
Reservation_SC_ST_Act1976-05-05legislationframeworkThe 1976 State law that reserves government jobs and posts for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in West Bengal (West Bengal Act XXVII of 1976). Later roster and register rules are made under it.
SCST-Identification-Act-19941994-08-03legislationframeworkThe 1994 State law on how Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe status is identified by certificate in West Bengal (West Bengal Act XXXVIII of 1994). It also covers cancelling false certificates.
6316-BCW2010-09-24gazette notificationframeworkModel 100-point roster for direct recruitment, issued in September 2010 to replace all earlier roster orders. It marks which of the 100 points go to SC, ST and OBC candidates.
3755-BCW2013-11-08gazette notificationframeworkBrings SC, ST and OBC certificates under the Right to Public Services Act, 2013. The SDO (in Kolkata, the District Welfare Officer) must issue the certificate within 4 weeks of a complete application.
2976-BCW2014-09-11commission and institutionalframeworkExplains how an adopted child of OBC parents can get an OBC certificate. The rule already used for adopted children of SC/ST parents applies in the same way.
3024-BCW2014-09-15commission and institutionalframeworkReconstitutes the West Bengal Commission for Backward Classes in September 2014, replacing the 2006 notification. Former Chief Justice Malay Sengupta is named chairperson, with three members.
182-BCW2015-01-16rtps amendmentframeworkAdds appeal deadlines to the 2013 certificate time-limit rule (3755-BCW). The District Magistrate must decide a first appeal, and the Joint Commissioner for Reservation a second appeal, within 4 weeks each.
No.960-BCW/MR-221/08 (Part-I)2015-03-13bcw memorandumframeworkHow to get an amended OBC certificate after Order 2770 of August 2014 renamed 24 OBC classes and Category-A/B was shown on certificates. Holders apply with the old certificate and fresh non-creamy-layer proof.
2205-BCW2015-06-11commission and institutionalframeworkSets Monday, Wednesday and Friday as the days when offices can visit the Commissioner for Reservation for help keeping their Register of Appointments under the SC/ST and OBC reservation laws.
No. 1204-SBCW/MR-67/102015-07-27bcw memorandumframeworkThe 2015 OBC certificate guidelines, replacing the 2010 memo (1464-BCW). An applicant must belong to a listed class and be outside the creamy layer (then income below Rs 6 lakh); applications are made online.
No.3376-BCW2015-08-24bcw memorandumframeworkNames the Backward Classes Welfare Officer in Kolkata to hold hearings for certificate applicants who lack documents, as the 2015 SC/ST guidelines require. Effective from 24 August 2015.
4977-BCW2015-12-18gazette notificationframeworkHow roster points in the Register of Appointments are adjusted when staff move between similar offices with different appointing authorities. A vacancy left by a transfer may be filled by transfer or fresh recruitment.
No. 921-BCW/MR-88/142017-03-21bcw memorandumframeworkAadhaar is accepted as proof of both identity and local residence for SC, ST and OBC certificates. It is added to the document lists in the 2015 SC/ST and OBC guidelines.
No. 2252 (68)-BCW/MR-39/172017-07-04bcw memorandumframeworkIssued after applicants were found changing their names or surnames to get certificates. Officers must check original school records, especially the Madhyamik (or equivalent) certificate.
325-PAR_EWS2019-07-09ews frameworkframeworkWest Bengal's basic order on EWS (economically weaker sections) reservation after the 103rd Constitution Amendment. It gives 10% reservation in direct recruitment and in admissions.
BCW-150112022-10-21bcw memorandumframeworkTime limits for complaints about caste certificates made on the online single-window portal. The SDO (in Kolkata, the District Welfare Officer) must resolve a complaint within 15 working days.
No.BCW-22099/6/2022-SECTION(BCW)-Dept. of BCW/5037762022-10-21bcw memorandumframeworkDigitally signed (e-signed) caste certificates extended to every sub-division in West Bengal, after a pilot in a few sub-divisions and Kolkata in September 2022.
3869-BCW2022-11-16welfare outreachframeworkAsks District Magistrates to tell SC/ST students in Classes XI and XII about NIFT degree courses, because seats reserved for them were going unfilled every year.
1152-L2022-11-28legislation amendmentframework2022 amendment to the SC/ST Identification Act, 1994. New Section 9A lets a person appeal to the District Magistrate if the SDO cancels or takes back their certificate; before this there was no appeal in law.
959-BCW2023-05-18ews frameworkframeworkChanges how EWS certificates are issued, building on the 2019 EWS order. The Additional District Magistrate (in Kolkata, the District Welfare Officer) now issues them, and Aadhaar or Khadya Sathi card and PAN are required.
