  3. 13 Jul 1994

    Founding reservation order: 5% for Backward Classes, with a creamy-layer schedule

    Order No. 347-TW/EC, dated 13 July 1994 (Scheduled Castes & Tribes Welfare Department), made the first reservation of 5% of vacancies filled by direct recruitment in state government service, local/statutory bodies, government-majority corporations, universities, aided schools, and the public sector, in favour of Backward Classes notified under the West Bengal Commission for Backward Classes Act, 1993. Clause (b) attached a detailed exclusion Schedule (Categories I–VI: constitutional posts, Class I/II officers, armed forces, professional class, property owners, and an income/wealth test then set at Rs. 2.5 lakh/year) — an early version of what later came to be called the "creamy layer" test. This order was later amended to raise the percentage (see 6 Nov 1997) and was still being cited as the source of the creamy-layer schedule in certificates issued in 2001.

    Related: 346-TW/EC list of 13.7.1994, 374(71)-TW/EC certificate instructions, 1056-BCW/EC 7% increase.

  4. 13 Jul 1994

    First OBC list notified: 14 classes

    Notification No. 346-TW/EC, dated 13 July 1994, issued the same day as the reservation order, specified the founding list of Backward Classes under section 2(a)/2(c) of the 1993 Act: Kapali, Baishya Kapali, Kurmi, Sutradhar, Karmakar, Kumbhakar, Swarnakar, Teli, Napit, Yogi-Nath, Goala-Gope, Moira-Modak (Halwai), Barujibi, Satchasi. Every later addition notification (see entries below) is framed as "further" to this list.

    Related: 347-TW/EC reservation order, 92-TW/EC 1997 amendment to the Goala-Gope entry.

  5. 27 Jul 1994

    Certificate machinery set up: SDOs as issuing authority

    Circular No. 374(71)-TW/EC/MR-103/94, dated 27 July 1994, circulated the two 13 July orders to all District Magistrates and Sub-Divisional Officers and directed that reservation be maintained in direct recruitment as specified. It made the Sub-Divisional Officer of the concerned sub-division the competent authority to issue Backward Classes certificates (District Magistrate, South 24-Parganas, for Calcutta town), applying the existing SC/ST certificate procedure mutatis mutandis. It enclosed the first application form and certificate proforma.

  20. 25 Mar 1999

    SC/ST model roster revised — BC points folded into the "General" slots

    Order No. 134-BCW/RC, dated 25 March 1999 (Reservation Cell), superseded all previous rosters and prescribed a fresh SC/ST 100-point model roster under the West Bengal SC/ST (Reservation of Vacancies) Act, 1976. Paragraph 2 directed that within this roster, the 9th, 19th, 29th, 49th, 59th, 69th, and 89th vacancies shown as "General" be reserved for Backward Classes — i.e., the BC quota was implemented as carve-outs inside the combined roster rather than a wholly separate one.

  21. 27 May 1999

    Reference date for permanent residence fixed at 15 March 1993

    Memorandum No. 1228-BCW, dated 27 May 1999, resolved confusion among certificate-issuing authorities by fixing 15 March 1993 (the date the West Bengal Commission for Backward Classes was notified) as the reference date for determining permanent residential status of non-SC/ST Backward Class applicants. A person residing permanently in the state "on or before" that date qualifies, subject to belonging to a notified class and meeting other criteria. (The words "on or before" were substituted for an original "and from" by the corrigendum below.)

    Related: 944-BCW corrigendum, 28 Feb 2000.

  22. 27 Jul 1999

    No de-reservation without State Government sanction

    Notification No. 2004-BCW/MRD-238/98, dated 27 July 1999, laid down that a vacancy reserved for Backward Classes must ordinarily remain unfilled if no qualified BC candidate is available, rather than being de-reserved by the appointing authority. De-reservation is possible only on reference to, and by order of, the State Government, and even then the reservation must be carried forward to the next unreserved vacancy.

  28. 21 Jun 2001

    Merit-qualifying BC candidates not deducted from the reserved quota

    Order No. 2489-BCW/MR-389/99, dated 21 June 2001, directed that when computing the reserved vacancies available to Backward Classes, candidates from that class who qualify on merit for an unreserved vacancy shall not be deducted from the reserved quota for that post — i.e., BC merit selections are treated as additional to, not a substitute for, the reserved seats.

  76. 2026-07-15

    2012 reservation Act amended

    658-L / West Bengal Act VIII of 2026. The amendment revises the statutory definitions, reservation and sub-categorisation provisions, certificate requirements and schedules, and omits section 16. Percentage-setting and sub-categorisation require consultation with the Commission. Published on 15 July; brought into force on 27 July 2026 by BCW/15011/17/1579. The amendment does not itself publish a new class list or restore 17% reservation.
